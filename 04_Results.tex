\section{Summarized results from Study 1}
Due to its flat vector nature, an immediate property of Phylo2Vec is its compressiveness. Compared to a Newick string, saving/transferring topologies as a Phylo2Vec can be up to x\% more efficient depending on the floating precision, and y\% for trees with branch lengths as a matrix. Whereas this is not a major concern for everyday phylogenetic tasks, this can constitute some significant improvement for large trees ($\approx$ 10,000 taxa).
